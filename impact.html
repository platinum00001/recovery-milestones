Community impact

Aggregate totals for funders and partners. No personal information is ever shown here.

How we protect privacy in these numbers

  • Only totals are shown — no names, dates, towns or individual records.
  • Any count under 10 is shown as “<10” (small-number suppression) so no one can be identified.
  • “Supporters engaged” counts sponsors, friends and family members who opted in to cheer someone on.

Funders who need more detail can request an aggregate report from roottorisema@gmail.com.
