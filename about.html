About Recovery Milestones

Recovery Milestones is like birthday rewards, but for recovery.

Based in Falmouth, Massachusetts, and serving Cape Cod and nearby communities, Recovery Milestones helps people in recovery mark the days that matter — 24 hours, 30, 60 and 90 days, 6 and 9 months, a year, and every year after — with privacy first.

What it does

  • Counts your days privately and shows your next milestone.
  • Unlocks small rewards from local businesses that want to celebrate with you.
  • Gives you a badge wall and a shareable celebration card — only if you want to share.
  • Lets sponsors, friends and family cheer you on, with notes you approve.
  • Connects you with peer recovery coaches.

How it's funded

Recovery Milestones is free for members and for businesses posting rewards. Optional featured sponsor spots and grants help keep Recovery Milestones free. We report only aggregate, privacy-protected totals to funders.
