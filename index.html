🌱 Track privately

Add your clean date. We count the days and show your next milestone: 24 hours, 30, 60 and 90 days, 6 and 9 months, 1 year and every year after. Your date stays private unless you choose to share it.

🎁 Get local rewards

Coffee shops, bakeries, gyms and more offer milestone rewards. Claim a code, show it at the counter — that's it. No one needs proof of anything beyond the code.

💚 Feel the support

Collect milestone badges on your wall, send a celebration card to Facebook if you like, and invite a sponsor or friend to get a heads-up so they can leave you a note.

Your privacy, plainly

  • Your sobriety date is private by default. Nobody — not businesses, not other members — sees it unless you opt in.
  • Businesses only see a reward code and which milestone it was for. Never your name, email or date.
  • We never sell your data, never show ads, and never use tracking scripts.
  • Export or delete everything, any time, from Settings.
